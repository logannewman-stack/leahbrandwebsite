success that costs you yourself isn't success. Leah helps people lead themselves first,
so they can lead their families, teams and businesses well.

- **Primary tagline:** Become who you were made to be.
- **Show tagline:** From potential to proof.
- **Work tagline:** Lead yourself first.
- **Signature line (her words):** "The wrong people want to be inspired. The right people want to be changed."

## 2. Positioning

**For** high-achieving business owners, founders and leaders, many of them in dentistry,
who have built real success but feel quietly disconnected from their own lives,

**Leah Roling is** the Master Certified Coach, author and speaker who installs
*self-leadership*, the internal operating system underneath every result,

**so that** they and their teams can grow without sacrificing who they are.

**Unlike** motivational speakers and tactics-first consultants, Leah works on the operator
underneath the operation: how people think, decide, and hold themselves accountable.
She is the coach who will say the thing nobody else will say.

## 7. Voice & messaging

**How she sounds:** short sentences. Pattern interrupts. A reframe, then a truth.
She uses contrast ("thermometer vs. thermostat"), names what people won't say out loud,
and always lands on what to *do* next.

**Do**
- Lead with a truth people feel but haven't said yet.
- Use contrast pairs: *build a life / live one*, *inspired / changed*.
- Say less. Short lines, white space.
- End with an invitation to act.

**Don't**
- Hype, exclamation points, or "unlock your best self" clichés.
- Jargon without a human translation.
- Shame. The truth is always told *for* the person, never *at* them.

**Signature lines (from Leah's own work)**
- "You were taught how to build a life. You were never taught how to live one."
- "Your people aren't overwhelmed. They are under-clear."
- "Culture is the strategy. The rest is decoration."
- "My worth was never theirs to set."
- "There are no balls. No one is juggling."
- "Life takes reps."

## 10. Visual identity

### Logo suite: "The Doorway"
- **Wordmark:** *Leah* in italic, Roling in roman. The italic is the person; the roman is the authority.
- **Monogram:** an italic *L* and roman *R* joined into a ligature, framed by an arch.
  The arch is a **doorway**, from Leah's own line "One philosophy. Many doorways."
  It is also a cathedral window (faith) and the shape of home (family).
- **Keystone:** the small diamond at the top of the arch. The keystone is the stone that holds
  the whole arch together. For Leah, that's her faith.
- **Seal:** the monogram ringed by *LEAH ROLING • COACH • AUTHOR • SPEAKER*.
- **Signature:** a handwritten *Leah*, used for sign-offs, book plates and personal notes.

Clear space = the height of the keystone × 4 on every side. Minimum size: wordmark 120 px /
1.25 in wide; monogram 28 px / 0.3 in tall.

### Typography
- **Fraunces** (display serif): headlines, pull quotes, the wordmark. Soft, warm, editorial.
  Use the italic for the *one word that matters*.
- **Instrument Sans** (sans): body copy, UI, labels. Small caps labels tracked +24%.
- **Mrs Saint Delafield** (script): signature only. Never for sentences.

Both Fraunces and Instrument Sans are free (Google Fonts / SIL Open Font License).

### Photography
- Real, warm, natural light. Leah laughing, mid-conversation, on stage, with people.
- One black-and-white hero portrait for gravitas, then warm color everywhere else.
- Wardrobe that carries the palette: black, cream, and her signature reds.
- Avoid: stock-photo handshakes, staged "thinking" poses, heavy filters.

### Graphic motifs
- Arch-cropped portraits (the doorway)
- Hairline rules and small-caps labels, like an editorial magazine
- One italic word per headline in Garnet
- The keystone diamond as a bullet or section marker
