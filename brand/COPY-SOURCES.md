# Website copy: where every line comes from

The site is written in Leah's first-person voice. Most of it is her own published
wording. This file marks each part so she can approve it quickly.

- **Hers** = copied word for word from something she published.
- **Hers, edited** = her sentence with small changes (a dash turned into a full stop,
  two of her lines joined, a line shortened).
- **Written for her** = new wording in her style. She has not said these. Approve or replace.

Sources: her current site (leahrolingcoaching.com: home, come-closer, about, dentists,
contact), innerboardos.com, innerboardsuite.com, the podcast feed for *The Becoming You
Show* (207 episodes as of 13 Nov 2026), and the Goodreads listings for her books.

## Voice rules the copy follows

- Short declarative sentences and fragments. Second person.
- Contrast pairs: "Not X. Y." and "You don't have an X problem. You have a Y problem."
- She names what you are avoiding, then gives one small next step.
- Blunt, always paired with reassurance. She never calls the reader broken.
- No gurus, rescue, fixes, hacks or hustle. Almost no exclamation marks.
- Her words: the work, becoming, worth, permission, operating system, under-clear,
  reps, proof, doorways, the room, ordinary Tuesday, have your own back.

## Flags for Leah

1. **First person.** The site now speaks as "I". Everything marked *Written for her* is
   a sentence she has not said.
2. **Her father.** The story section uses what she published about her father on her
   own site. Confirm she wants it here too.
3. **Faith.** I found no place where she states her faith publicly in her own words,
   beyond a light mention in early episodes. The faith lines and the verse were written
   and chosen for her.
4. **Numbers that differ between her own pages.** Years: "15+ years coaching" (used
   here), "three decades", "twenty-plus years", and "25 years" on a conference bio.
   Clients: "hundreds of practice owners" and "thousands of clients".
5. **Book count.** The site says four books, matching her speaker bio. Goodreads also
   lists *Your Guide to Living Lighter* (2023).
6. **Credentials.** Her pages list different sets. The site uses the dentists-page set:
   MCC, CHPC, John Maxwell, CBT, neurolinguistics, 8+ certifications. "Organizational
   psychology" was removed because only a third-party bio mentions it.
7. **Removed claims.** "81% less absenteeism" and "18% lower turnover" came from a
   third-party podcast page, not from her. They are no longer on the site.
8. **A line that is not hers.** "You can't be a good person when you're in a hurry" is
   an episode title, but her notes say she heard it from someone else. It is not used
   as a quote.
9. **Episode count.** Her site says 183. The feed is at 207, so the site says "200+".
10. **Book credits.** Goodreads lists both: foreword by Craig Jackman, afterword by
    Tim O'Donnell. Both are shown.
