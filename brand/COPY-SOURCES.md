# Website copy: where every line comes from

The site is written in Leah's first-person voice. Most of it is her own published
wording. This file marks each part so she can approve it quickly.

- **Hers** = copied word for word from something she published.
- **Hers, edited** = her sentence with small changes (a dash turned into a full stop,
  two of her lines joined, a line shortened).
- **Written for her** = new wording in her style. She has not said these. Approve or replace.

Sources: her current site (leahrolingcoaching.com: home, come-closer, about, work,
dentists, contact), innerboardos.com, innerboardsuite.com, the podcast feed for
*The Becoming You Show* (207 episodes as of 13 Nov 2026), and the Goodreads listings
for her books.

## Voice rules the copy follows

- Short declarative sentences and fragments. Second person.
- Contrast pairs: "Not X. Y." and "You don't have an X problem. You have a Y problem."
- She names what you are avoiding, then gives one small next step.
- Blunt, always paired with reassurance. She never calls the reader broken.
- No gurus, rescue, fixes, hacks or hustle. Almost no exclamation marks.
- Her words: the work, becoming, worth, permission, operating system, under-clear,
  reps, proof, doorways, the room, ordinary Tuesday, have your own back.

## Flags for Leah

1. **First person.** The site speaks as "I". Everything marked *Written for her* is a
   sentence she has not said.
2. **Her story.** The story is a shortened version of what she published on her own
   site. The part about her father is left out of this version. Say if she wants it back.
3. **STEAR.** The five names are hers. The one-line description under each letter was
   written for her and needs her eye most of all. She also has a tool called SPARK with
   nothing published about it yet, so it is not on the site.
4. **Faith.** I found no place where she states her faith publicly in her own words,
   beyond a light mention in early episodes. On this version faith appears only as the
   word "Believer" in the list under her story.
5. **Numbers that differ between her own pages.** Years: "15+ years coaching" (used
   here), "three decades", "twenty-plus years", and "25 years" on a conference bio.
   Clients: "hundreds of practice owners" and "thousands of clients".
6. **Credentials.** Her pages list different sets. The site names three from her
   dentists page: Master Certified Coach, Certified High Performance Coach, John
   Maxwell-trained.
7. **Removed claims.** "81% less absenteeism" and "18% lower turnover" came from a
   third-party podcast page, not from her. They are not on the site.
8. **Client quotes.** Two quotes with initials come from the "Client Stories" on her
   About page, shortened. Confirm she still has permission to use them.
9. **"Seen and heard at."** These are places that list her as a speaker or guest. They
   are set as plain text because no logo files or permissions exist. Swap in real logos
   only with permission.
10. **Episode numbers.** The three episodes shown were the newest on 13 Nov 2026. Update
    them now and then, or remove the row.
11. **Book credits.** Goodreads lists both: foreword by Craig Jackman, afterword by
    Tim O'Donnell. Both are shown.
