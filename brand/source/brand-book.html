02 — Voice

How she sounds.

Short sentences. Pattern interrupts. A reframe, then a truth.

She uses contrast to make a point land, names what people won’t say out loud, and always ends on what to do next.

Contrast pairs she uses
Build a life / live one
Inspired / changed
Thermometer / thermostat
Overwhelmed / under-clear
✓Do
Lead with a truth.
Say what people feel but haven’t put into words yet.
Use contrast.
“You were taught how to build a life. You were never taught how to live one.”
Say less.
Short lines and plenty of white space. Let the line breathe.
End with an invitation.
Every piece closes with one clear next step.
✕Don’t
Hype.
No exclamation points, no “unlock your best self!” clichés.
Hide in jargon.
Every framework gets a plain, human translation.
Shame.
The truth is always told for the person, never at them.
Over-explain.
If it needs three paragraphs, it needs a better first line.
Off-brand
Unlock your full potential with our amazing coaching program!
On-brand
You don’t need more motivation. You need more reps.

03 — Supporting marks

Monogram, seal & signature.

The Monogram

For small or square spaces where the full name won’t fit: favicons, social avatars, embossing, the top of a slide, a book spine.

The Seal

A finishing mark, not a lead logo. Use it for stickers, certificates, INQ graduation materials, merch, wax seals and the back of business cards.

The Signature

Leah’s personal sign-off for thank-you cards, book plates, newsletters and the last slide of a keynote. Never use the script for sentences.

03 — Logo misuse

Please don’t.

The fastest way to make a premium brand look cheap is to bend the logo. When in doubt, use the supplied files exactly as they are.

✕
Don’t stretch or squash.Always scale proportionally.
✕
Don’t recolor.Use only Garnet, Ink, Linen and Brass versions.
✕
Don’t rotate.The wordmark always sits level.
✕
Don’t add effects.No shadows, glows, outlines or bevels.
✕
Don’t place on busy images.Use a calm area or a solid brand color.
✕
Don’t rebuild the lockup.Use the supplied lockup, monogram on the left.
