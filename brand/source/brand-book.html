02 — Voice

How she sounds.

Short sentences. Pattern interrupts. A reframe, then a truth.

She uses contrast to make a point land, names what people won't say out loud, and always ends on what to do next.

Contrast pairs she uses

Build a life / live one Inspired / changed Thermometer / thermostat Overwhelmed / under-clear
✓Do
Lead with a truth.

Say what people feel but haven't put into words yet.

Use contrast.

"You were taught how to build a life. You were never taught how to live one."

Say less.

Short lines and plenty of white space. Let the line breathe.

End with an invitation.

Every piece closes with one clear next step.

×Don't
Hype.

No exclamation points, no "unlock your best self" clichés.

Hide in jargon.

Every framework gets a plain, human translation.

Shame.

The truth is always told for the person, never at them.

Over-explain.

If it needs three paragraphs, it needs a better first line.

Off-brand

Unlock your full potential with our amazing coaching program!

On-brand

You don't need more motivation. You need more reps.

03 — Primary wordmark

The primary signature

The wordmark is Leah's main logo. Use it on the website, book jackets, slides, stage screens and anywhere her name leads.

Lowercase on purpose

Lowercase reads warm, current and human. It says "come on in" before she says a word. Never retype it in title case.

Always use the artwork

The wordmark is custom-spaced and outlined. Never retype it in a font; use the files in brand/logos/.

03 — Supporting marks

Mark, seal & signature.

The mark

For small or square spaces where the full name won't fit: favicons, social avatars, app icons, the top of a slide, a book spine.

The seal

A finishing mark, never a lead logo. Use it for stickers, certificates, INQ graduation materials, merch and the back of business cards.

The signature

Leah's personal sign-off for thank-you cards, book plates, newsletters and the last slide of a keynote. Never use the script for sentences.

03 — Clear space & minimum size

Give it room to breathe.

2x 2x
x = the diameter of the sun dot

Keep at least 2x of empty space on every side of the wordmark and lockup. For the mark on its own, keep a quarter of its width. Text, photo edges and other logos stay outside this zone.

Minimum sizes

Wordmark

100 px digital · 1 in print

Lockup

140 px digital · 1.4 in print

Mark

24 px tall · 0.25 in print

Seal

64 px · 0.75 in (keep the ring text legible)

Below these sizes, switch to the mark.

03 — Logo misuse

Please don't.

The fastest way to make a fresh brand look cheap is to bend the logo. When in doubt, use the supplied files exactly as they are.

×
Don't recolor.

Only the approved Ink, Paper and Sunshine versions.

×
Don't stretch or squash.

Always scale proportionally.

×
Don't rotate.

The wordmark always sits level.

×
Don't add effects.

No shadows, glows, outlines or bevels.

×
Don't place on busy images.

Use a calm area of a photo or a solid brand color.

×
Don't rebuild the lockup.

Use the supplied lockup: mark on the left, wordmark on the right.

05 — Photography

Real, warm, alive.

Leah's photography should feel like you're in the room with her: laughing, mid-conversation, on stage, with people.

✓Do

Natural light and real moments · One black-and-white hero portrait for contrast · Bright, clean backgrounds · Wardrobe that plays with the palette: black, white, soft yellows and lilacs · Arch crops for portraits

×Avoid

Stock handshakes and staged "thinking" poses · Heavy filters and presets · Dark, moody lighting · Cluttered backgrounds behind text
