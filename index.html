Her story

For years, she was very good at earning love.

Through middle school, college and into her career, Leah carried a quiet conviction: if she achieved enough and pleased the right people, she'd finally have permission to live.

It worked, on paper. Underneath, she was exhausted. Resentful. Guilty. Quietly furious at a life she'd spent so much energy performing. The turning point wasn't a new strategy. It was a truth.

“My worth was never theirs to set.”

Leah Roling

She left 25 years of corporate work to build her own coaching practice and method. Today she walks other people across the same bridge she crossed: from the stuck self to the becoming self.

The philosophy

There are only two master skills.

Everything else is a derivative. Self-leadership isn't a leadership skill. It's a human one, and it can be learned.

01

Personal Power

Staying grounded and self-directed no matter what's happening around you. You lead your thoughts, emotions and choices instead of being led by them.

02

Social Influence

Leading a room through presence and inner steadiness, not technique. When you're settled, people around you settle too.

A thermometer reads the temperature. A thermostat sets it.

Leah's work turns leaders into thermostats: people who set the culture instead of reacting to it.
