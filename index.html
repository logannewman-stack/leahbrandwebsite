How STEAR works.

STEAR is the five-part model for how a high performing leader steers their own thinking, energy, and action before steering anyone else's.

  • Before you can lead a practice,
  • lead a team,
  • or lead through uncertainty,
  • you have to lead yourself.
  1. S

    Situation

    What happened. Just the facts. No story attached yet.

  2. T

    Thoughts

    The sentence in your mind about it. This is where the work is.

  3. E

    Emotions

    What that thought creates in you. Feelings are information, not instructions.

  4. A

    Action

    What you do, or don't do, from there. Follow through is a yes or a no.

  5. R

    Results

    What your actions produce. Results don't lie.

Self-leadership is not a leadership skill. It is a human one.
